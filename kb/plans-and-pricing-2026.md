# Fernway plans and pricing (2026)

*Version 2026.1 · Effective 1 June 2026 · Status: current*

## Plans at a glance

Fernway has three plans: Starter for small teams getting organised, Team for growing agencies, and Business for larger agencies that need more control. Prices are per seat.

**Plans at a glance (prices in USD per seat per month)**

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing | Seats | Active projects | Storage | Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $9 | $7 | 1 to 5 | 10 | 20 GB | Email, within 2 business days |
| Team | $19 | $15 | 3 to 50 | Unlimited | 1 TB | Email and chat, within 1 business day |
| Business | $35 | $29 | 10 or more | Unlimited | 5 TB | Priority, within 4 hours |

## Annual and monthly billing

You can pay monthly or annually. Annual billing is charged once a year; the annual price is shown per seat per month. Annual billing costs less per seat on every plan.

## Free trial

New workspaces get a 14-day free trial of the Team plan, and no credit card is required. At the end of the trial, choose a plan to keep working.

## Features by plan

- The Business plan has a minimum of 10 seats.
- Business workspaces with 25 or more seats get a named customer success manager.
- SAML single sign-on and SCIM provisioning are available on the Business plan only.
- The audit log is a Business plan feature and keeps events for 365 days.
- Project budgets are available on the Team and Business plans.
