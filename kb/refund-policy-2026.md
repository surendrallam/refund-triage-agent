# Refund policy (2026)

*Version 2.0 · Effective 1 March 2026 · Status: current*

## Scope

This policy covers refunds of Fernway subscription payments on every plan. This policy took effect on 1 March 2026 and replaces the Refund policy (2025).

## Annual plans

Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of the start of the annual term. After those 30 days, annual plans are not refundable, but you can turn off auto-renewal at any time.

## Monthly plans

On monthly plans, the most recent monthly charge can be refunded if you ask within 7 days of that charge.

## Seat additions

Seats added to an annual plan can be refunded within 30 days of being added.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

## How to request a refund

To ask for a refund, contact support from Help in the app and include the invoice number.
