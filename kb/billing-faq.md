# Billing FAQ

## Payment methods

Fernway accepts Visa, Mastercard and American Express cards. Annual Business plans can also pay by bank transfer against an invoice with 30-day payment terms.

## Currencies and tax

You can be billed in US dollars (USD), euros (EUR) or pounds sterling (GBP). VAT is added for customers in the EU unless a valid VAT ID is entered in billing settings.

## Invoices

Invoices are emailed to the billing contact and can be downloaded under Settings, Billing, Invoices. Keep the billing contact up to date so invoices reach the right person.

## Failed payments

If a card payment fails, Fernway retries it 3 times over 7 days, on days 1, 3 and 7. If the last retry fails, the workspace becomes read-only until the balance is paid. A workspace that stays unpaid for 30 days after becoming read-only is suspended.

## Changing seats or plans

Added seats are charged immediately, prorated to the end of the billing period. Removed seats and plan downgrades take effect at the next renewal.
